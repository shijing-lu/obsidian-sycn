(1) 
$$
\int F'(x) \, dx = F(x) + C \quad \text{或} \quad \int dF(x) = F(x) + C.
$$

(2) 
$$
\left[ \int f(x) \, dx \right]' = f(x) \quad \text{或} \quad d\left[ \int f(x) \, dx \right] = f(x) \, dx.
$$

(3) 设函数 $f(x), g(x)$ 的原函数存在，$k, l$ 为非零常数，则
$$
\int [k f(x) + l g(x)] \, dx = k \int f(x) \, dx + l \int g(x) \, dx.
$$
