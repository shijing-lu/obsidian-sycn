函数 $z=f(x, y)$ 在区域 $D$ 内的偏导数 $f_x^{\prime}(x, y)$ 和 $f_y^{\prime}(x, y)$ 仍然是 $x, y$ 的二元函数，如果 $f_x^{\prime}(x, y)$ 和 $f_y^{\prime}(x, y)$ 在区域 $D$ 内仍具有偏导数，则称它们的偏导数为函数 $z=f(x, y)$ 的二阶偏导数，记作
$$
\begin{gathered}
f_{xx}^{\prime\prime}(x, y)=\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\right)=\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2},\quad f_{xy}^{\prime\prime}(x, y)=\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\right)=\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x\partial y}\\
f_{yx}^{\prime\prime}(x, y)=\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\right)=\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y\partial x},\quad f_{yy}^{\prime\prime}(x, y)=\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\right)=\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2}
\end{gathered}
$$
其中 $\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x\partial y}$ 和 $\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y\partial x}$ 称为二阶混合偏导数。类似地，可以定义更高阶的偏导数。
