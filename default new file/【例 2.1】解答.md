**证明**（泰勒展开法）：
在 $x_0$ 处将 $f(x_0 + h)$ 和 $f(x_0 + 2h)$ 展开到二阶：
$$
f(x_0 + h) = f(x_0) + f'(x_0) h + \frac{1}{2} f''(x_0) h^2 + o(h^2),
$$
$$
f(x_0 + 2h) = f(x_0) + 2f'(x_0) h + 2 f''(x_0) h^2 + o(h^2).
$$
代入分子：
$$
\begin{aligned}
& f(x_0 + 2h) - 2f(x_0 + h) + f(x_0) \\
&= \bigl[f(x_0) + 2f'(x_0)h + 2f''(x_0)h^2 + o(h^2)\bigr] \\
&\quad - 2\bigl[f(x_0) + f'(x_0)h + \tfrac12 f''(x_0)h^2 + o(h^2)\bigr] + f(x_0) \\
&= f''(x_0) h^2 + o(h^2).
\end{aligned}
$$
因此
$$
\frac{f(x_0 + 2h) - 2f(x_0 + h) + f(x_0)}{h^2}
= f''(x_0) + \frac{o(h^2)}{h^2} \to f''(x_0) \quad (h \to 0).
$$
得证：
$$
\boxed{\lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x_0 + 2h) - 2f(x_0 + h) + f(x_0)}{h^2} = f''(x_0)}.
$$
