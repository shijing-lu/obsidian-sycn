**解**：令 $t = x-2$，则 $x = t+2$，$dx = dt$。当 $x=1$ 时 $t=-1$，$x=3$ 时 $t=1$。于是
$$
\int_{1}^{3} f(x-2) \, dx = \int_{-1}^{1} f(t) \, dt = \int_{-1}^{0} (1+t^2) \, dt + \int_{0}^{1} e^{-t} \, dt.
$$
计算得：
$$
\int_{-1}^{0} (1+t^2) \, dt = \left[ t + \frac{t^3}{3} \right]_{-1}^{0} = 0 - \left( -1 + \frac{-1}{3} \right) = 1 + \frac{1}{3} = \frac{4}{3},
$$
$$
\int_{0}^{1} e^{-t} \, dt = \left[ -e^{-t} \right]_{0}^{1} = -e^{-1} + 1 = 1 - \frac{1}{e}.
$$
因此
$$
\int_{1}^{3} f(x-2) \, dx = \frac{4}{3} + 1 - \frac{1}{e} = \frac{7}{3} - \frac{1}{e}.
$$
$\boxed{\dfrac{7}{3} - \dfrac{1}{e}}$
