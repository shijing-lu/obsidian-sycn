## 3. 常用的高阶导数公式

$$
(a^x)^{(n)} = a^x (\ln a)^n, \quad (e^{ax})^{(n)} = a^n e^{ax}
$$

$$
\left( \frac{1}{ax+b} \right)^{(n)} = \frac{(-a)^n n!}{(ax+b)^{n+1}}
$$
$$
\left[ \ln(ax + b) \right]^{(n)} = \left( \frac{a}{ax + b} \right)^{(n-1)} = \frac{(-1)^{n-1} a^n (n-1)!}{(ax + b)^n}
$$

$$
(x^\alpha)^{(n)} = \alpha(\alpha-1)\cdots(\alpha-n+1) x^{\alpha-n}, \quad \alpha \in \mathbb{R}, \; n \in \mathbb{N}^*
$$

(3) 
$$
\left[ \sin(ax + b) \right]^{(n)} = a^n \sin\left(ax + b + \frac{n\pi}{2}\right); \quad (\sin x)^{(n)} = \sin\left(x + \frac{n\pi}{2}\right)
$$

(4) 
$$
\left[ \cos(ax + b) \right]^{(n)} = a^n \cos\left(ax + b + \frac{n\pi}{2}\right); \quad (\cos x)^{(n)} = \cos\left(x + \frac{n\pi}{2}\right)
$$
