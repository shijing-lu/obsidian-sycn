**解**：令 $I = \int_0^{\pi} x \sin^4 x \, dx$
由区间再现公式：
$$
I = \int_0^{\pi} (\pi - x) \sin^4(\pi - x) \, dx = \int_0^{\pi} (\pi - x) \sin^4 x \, dx
$$
两式相加：
$$
2I = \int_0^{\pi} \pi \sin^4 x \, dx = \pi \int_0^{\pi} \sin^4 x \, dx
$$
所以：
$$
I = \frac{\pi}{2} \int_0^{\pi} \sin^4 x \, dx = \frac{\pi}{2} \cdot 2 \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^4 x \, dx = \pi \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^4 x \, dx
$$
利用公式 $\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^n x \, dx = \frac{n-1}{n} \cdot \frac{n-3}{n-2} \cdots \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{\pi}{2}$（$n$ 为偶数）：
$$
\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^4 x \, dx = \frac{3}{4} \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{\pi}{2} = \frac{3\pi}{16}
$$
故：
$$
\boxed{I = \pi \cdot \frac{3\pi}{16} = \frac{3\pi^2}{16}}
$$
