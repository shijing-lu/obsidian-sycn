**正态分布的标准化**：若 $X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$，则 $Z=\frac{X-\mu}{\sigma}\sim N(0,1)$。于是有
      $$
      F(x)=P\{X\leq x\}=P\left\{\frac{X-\mu}{\sigma}\leq\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\right\}=\Phi\left(\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\right),
      $$
      $$
      P\{a<X\leq b\}=P\left\{\frac{a-\mu}{\sigma}<\frac{X-\mu}{\sigma}\leq\frac{b-\mu}{\sigma}\right\}=\Phi\left(\frac{b-\mu}{\sigma}\right)-\Phi\left(\frac{a-\mu}{\sigma}\right).
      $$
