3) 运算规律与性质
① 交换律：$\boldsymbol{a} \cdot \boldsymbol{b} = \boldsymbol{b} \cdot \boldsymbol{a}$
② 分配律：$(\boldsymbol{a} + \boldsymbol{b}) \cdot \boldsymbol{c} = \boldsymbol{a} \cdot \boldsymbol{c} + \boldsymbol{b} \cdot \boldsymbol{c}$
③ 结合律：$(\lambda \boldsymbol{a}) \cdot \boldsymbol{b} = \lambda (\boldsymbol{a} \cdot \boldsymbol{b})$，其中 $\lambda$ 为实数
④ $\boldsymbol{a} \cdot \boldsymbol{a} = |\boldsymbol{a}|^2$，$|\boldsymbol{a}| = \sqrt{\boldsymbol{a} \cdot \boldsymbol{a}}$
⑤ 两个非零向量 $\boldsymbol{a} \perp \boldsymbol{b} \iff \boldsymbol{a} \cdot \boldsymbol{b} = 0$。即若 $x_1x_2 + y_1y_2 + z_1z_2 = 0$
⑥ 求两向量夹角：$\cos\theta = \frac{\boldsymbol{a} \cdot \boldsymbol{b}}{|\boldsymbol{a}||\boldsymbol{b}|} = \frac{x_1x_2 + y_1y_2 + z_1z_2}{\sqrt{x_1^2+y_1^2+z_1^2} \cdot \sqrt{x_2^2+y_2^2+z_2^2}}$

注：$\cos^2\alpha + \cos^2\beta + \cos^2\gamma = 1$
