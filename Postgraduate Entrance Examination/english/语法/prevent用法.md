# prevent 用法详解

## 📚 基本含义

**prevent** /prɪˈvent/ vt. 阻止，防止，阻挡

## 🔧 语法结构

### 1. prevent + 宾语 + from + doing（主要结构）
```
prevent + 某人/物 + from + 动名词
```

**例句：**
- *The law prevents employers from firing workers without cause.*
- *The rain prevented us from going to the park.*
- *Safety measures prevent accidents from happening.*

### 2. prevent + 宾语 + from + 名词
```
prevent + 某人/物 + from + 名词
```

**例句：**
- *The wall prevents the noise from the street.*
- *Vaccines prevent people from the disease.*
- *The fence prevents animals from the garden.*

### 3. prevent + doing（较少使用）
```
prevent + 动名词
```

**例句：**
- *What prevented you from coming?*
- *Nothing can prevent us achieving our goal.*

## 📝 使用规则

### from 的用法
- **正式文体**：必须保留 from
  - *The regulations prevent companies from polluting.*
- **英式英语**：通常保留 from
- **美式英语**：有时可省略 from
  - *The law prevents employers firing workers.*

### 时态变化
| 时态 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| 一般现在时 | *The law prevents...* |
| 一般过去时 | *The rain prevented...* |
| 现在完成时 | *has prevented...* |
| 将来时 | *will prevent...* |

### 语态变化
- **主动语态**：*The law prevents employers from firing workers.*
- **被动语态**：*Employers are prevented from being fired.*

## 🔄 同义替换

### 相同结构
| 动词 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| stop | stop + 宾语 + from + doing | *stop employers from firing* |
| keep | keep + 宾语 + from + doing | *keep employers from firing* |
| discourage | discourage + 宾语 + from + doing | *discourage smoking* |

### 不同结构
| 动词 | 用法 |
|------|------|
| forbid | forbid + 宾语 + to do |
| prohibit | prohibit + 宾语 + from + doing |

## 🎯 考试重点

### 常见考点
1. **from 的保留与省略**
2. **doing 的形式**
3. **宾语的位置**
4. **时态和语态**

### 易错点
- ❌ *prevent employers to fire*（错误）
- ✅ *prevent employers from firing*（正确）

### 真题分析
**原句：** The personal grievance provisions of New Zealand's Employment Relations Act 2000 (ERA) prevent an employer from firing an employee without good cause.

**结构分析：**
- 主语：The personal grievance provisions
- 谓语：prevent
- 宾语：an employer
- 补语：from firing an employee without good cause

## 📚 相关短语

### 固定搭配
- **prevent...from...**：阻止...做...
- **prevent disease**：预防疾病
- **prevent accidents**：防止事故
- **prevent pollution**：防止污染

### 常用表达
- *prevent the spread of disease*
- *prevent environmental damage*
- *prevent financial losses*
