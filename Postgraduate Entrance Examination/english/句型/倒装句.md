# 倒装句专题

## 📚 倒装句概述

倒装句是英语中重要的语法现象，分为**完全倒装**和**部分倒装**两种。

## 🔄 完全倒装

### 1. 地点副词开头的倒装

**结构：** 地点副词 + be动词 + 主语

**例句：**
- *Here comes the bus.*
- *There goes the bell.*
- *Up went the rocket.*

### 2. Long gone 句型

**结构：** Long gone + are/were + the days/time + when从句

**原句：** Long gone are the days when a boss could simply give an employee contractual notice.

**语法解析：**
- **正常语序：** The days when a boss could simply give an employee contractual notice are long gone.
- **倒装语序：** Long gone are the days when a boss could simply give an employee contractual notice.

**使用要点：**
- 强调时间已经过去，表达时代变迁
- 用于正式文体和学术写作
- 主句用一般现在时，从句用过去时

**例句：**
- *Long gone are the days when students could graduate without effort.*
- *Long gone are the days when people valued quality over quantity.*
- *Long gone are the days when handwritten letters were common.*

### 3. 表语前置的倒装

**结构：** 表语 + be动词 + 主语

**例句：**
- *Gone are the days of handwritten letters.*
- *Present at the meeting were all the department heads.*
- *Standing in the corner was a tall man.*

## 🔄 部分倒装

### 1. 否定词开头的倒装

**结构：** 否定词 + 助动词 + 主语 + 谓语

**常见否定词：**
- *Never, Seldom, Rarely, Hardly, Scarcely, Little, Few*
- *Not only, No sooner, Not until*

**例句：**
- *Never have I seen such a beautiful sight.*
- *Seldom do we get such opportunities.*
- *Not only does he speak English, but he also speaks French.*

### 2. Only 开头的倒装

**结构：** Only + 状语 + 助动词 + 主语 + 谓语

**例句：**
- *Only then did I understand the problem.*
- *Only by working hard can you succeed.*
- *Only in this way can we solve the issue.*

### 3. So/Neither/Nor 开头的倒装

**结构：** So/Neither/Nor + 助动词 + 主语

**例句：**
- *So do I.* (我也是)
- *Neither can he.* (他也不能)
- *Nor will they.* (他们也不会)

## 🎯 考试重点

### 常见考点
1. **完全倒装 vs 部分倒装** 的区别
2. **Long gone 句型** 的识别和应用
3. **否定词开头** 的倒装结构
4. **Only 开头** 的倒装规则

### 易错点
- ❌ *Long gone is the days...* (主谓不一致)
- ✅ *Long gone are the days...* (正确)
- ❌ *Never I have seen...* (没有倒装)
- ✅ *Never have I seen...* (正确倒装)

### 真题分析
**原句：** Long gone are the days when a boss could simply give an employee contractual notice.

**考点：**
- 完全倒装结构
- 主谓一致 (are 对应 days)
- when 引导的定语从句

## 📚 练习题

### 选择题
1. _____ when we could travel freely without restrictions.
   A) Long gone is the days
   B) Long gone are the days
   C) Long gone the days are
   D) Long gone days are

2. _____ I realized the importance of time management.
   A) Only then I
   B) Only then did I
   C) Only I then
   D) Only then I did

### 答案
1. B) Long gone are the days
2. B) Only then did I
