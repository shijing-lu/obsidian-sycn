# 写作句型模板

## 🌟 开头段模板

### 1. 时代变迁类

**Long gone 句型：**
- *Long gone are the days when... Today, things have changed dramatically.*
- *Long gone are the days when students could... Now, they face completely different challenges.*
- *Long gone are the days when people... The reality is that...*

**应用示例：**
- *Long gone are the days when college graduates could easily find jobs. Today, competition is fierce.*
- *Long gone are the days when handwritten communication was the norm. Now, digital messages dominate.*

### 2. 问题提出类

**There is no denying that...**
- *There is no denying that... has become a serious problem.*
- *There is no denying that we are facing...*

**In recent years, ... has aroused wide concern.**
- *In recent years, environmental pollution has aroused wide concern.*
- *In recent years, the issue of... has attracted public attention.*

## 🔄 主体段模板

### 1. 原因分析类

**There are several reasons for this phenomenon.**
- *First and foremost,...*
- *In addition,...*
- *Last but not least,...*

**The reasons are manifold.**
- *On the one hand,...*
- *On the other hand,...*
- *Furthermore,...*

### 2. 举例论证类

**Take... as an example.**
- *Take smartphone addiction as an example.*
- *Take environmental protection as an example.*

**A case in point is...**
- *A case in point is the phenomenon of online shopping.*
- *A case in point is the development of artificial intelligence.*

## 📈 对比段模板

### 1. 古今对比

**Long gone are the days when...**
- *Long gone are the days when people valued quality over quantity. Now, speed and efficiency are prioritized.*
- *Long gone are the days when handwritten letters were common. Today, emails and instant messages prevail.*

**Compared with the past,...**
- *Compared with the past, modern life is more convenient but also more stressful.*
- *Compared with the past, students today have more opportunities but also more pressure.*

### 2. 利弊对比

**While... has its advantages, it also brings problems.**
- *While technology has improved our lives, it has also created new challenges.*
- *While economic development is important, environmental protection cannot be ignored.*

**On the positive side,... However, on the negative side,...**
- *On the positive side, online education is convenient. However, on the negative side, it lacks personal interaction.*

## 🎯 结论段模板

### 1. 总结观点类

**In conclusion,...**
- *In conclusion, we must adapt to the new reality.*
- *In conclusion, both sides should be taken into consideration.*

**To sum up,...**
- *To sum up, the benefits outweigh the drawbacks.*
- *To sum up, a balanced approach is needed.*

### 2. 呼吁行动类

**It is high time that we...**
- *It is high time that we took action to solve this problem.*
- *It is high time that we reconsidered our approach.*

**We should...**
- *We should take immediate measures to address this issue.*
- *We should work together to find a solution.*

## 💡 高级句型

### 1. 强调句型

**It is... that...**
- *It is hard work that leads to success.*
- *It is education that changes people's lives.*

**What matters most is...**
- *What matters most is the quality of education.*
- *What matters most is people's attitude.*

### 2. 倒装句型

**Long gone are the days when...**
- *Long gone are the days when we could ignore environmental issues.*
- *Long gone are the days when traditional methods were sufficient.*

**Only when... can we...**
- *Only when we work together can we solve this problem.*
- *Only when we take action can we see results.*

**Not only... but also...**
- *Not only does technology improve efficiency, but it also creates new job opportunities.*
- *Not only should we focus on economic growth, but we should also protect the environment.*

## 📝 应用练习

### 题目：网络购物的利弊

**开头段：**
*Long gone are the days when people had to visit physical stores for shopping. Today, online shopping has revolutionized the way we purchase goods.*

**主体段：**
*On the positive side, online shopping offers convenience and variety. However, on the negative side, it raises concerns about product quality and personal data security.*

**结论段：**
*In conclusion, while online shopping has its advantages, consumers should be cautious and make informed decisions.*
