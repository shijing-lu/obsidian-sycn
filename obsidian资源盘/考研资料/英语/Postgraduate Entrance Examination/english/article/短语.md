# 英语短语和语法结构

## 📚 动词短语

### prevent 系列
- **prevent + 宾语 + from + doing**：阻止某人做某事
  - *The law prevents employers from firing workers without cause.*
  - *The rain prevented us from going out.*
  - *Safety measures prevent accidents from happening.*

- **prevent + 宾语 + from + 名词**：阻止某人/某事...
  - *The wall prevents the noise from the street.*
  - *Vaccines prevent people from the disease.*

### 相关结构
- **stop + 宾语 + from + doing**：阻止（同义替换）
- **keep + 宾语 + from + doing**：使...不做
- **discourage + 宾语 + from + doing**：劝阻

## 📖 使用要点

### from 的省略
- **正式文体**：通常保留 from
  - *The law prevents employers from firing workers.*
- **非正式文体**：可以省略 from
  - *The law prevents employers firing workers.*

### 被动语态
- **主动**：*The law prevents employers from firing workers.*
- **被动**：*Workers are prevented from being fired without cause.*

## 🎯 考试重点

### 常见考点
1. **from 的保留与省略**
2. **doing 的形式变化**
3. **宾语的位置**

### 真题例句
- *The personal grievance provisions prevent an employer from firing an employee without good cause.*
- *New regulations prevent companies from polluting the environment.*
