# 好句积累

## 🌟 倒装句式

### Long gone are the days 句型

**原句：** Long gone are the days when a boss could simply give an employee contractual notice.

**句型结构：** Long gone are the days when + 从句

**中文意思：** 老板可以简单通知就解雇员工的时代已经一去不复返了。

**语法解析：**
- 这是完全倒装句
- 正常语序：The days when a boss could simply give an employee contractual notice are long gone.
- 倒装后：Long gone are the days when a boss could simply give an employee contractual notice.

**使用场景：**
- 表达对过去时代的怀念或否定
- 强调时代变迁
- 用于议论文开头或转折

**类似表达：**
- *Gone are the days when...*
- *Those were the days when...*
- *The time has passed when...*

## 📚 拓展例句

### 基础用法
- *Long gone are the days when students could graduate without studying.*
- *Long gone are the days when people wrote letters by hand.*
- *Long gone are the days when we had to carry cash everywhere.*

### 考研写作应用
- *Long gone are the days when college graduates could easily find jobs.*
- *Long gone are the days when people valued quality over quantity.*
- *Long gone are the days when environmental protection was ignored.*

### 商务语境
- *Long gone are the days when companies could ignore customer feedback.*
- *Long gone are the days when traditional marketing was sufficient.*
- *Long gone are the days when employees stayed with one company for life.*

## 🎯 使用技巧

### 写作应用
**开头段：**
- *Long gone are the days when... Today, things have changed dramatically.*

**对比段：**
- *Long gone are the days when... Now, we face completely different challenges.*

**结论段：**
- *Long gone are the days when... We must adapt to the new reality.*

### 注意事项
1. **时态一致**：主句用一般现在时，从句用过去时
2. **逻辑清晰**：确保对比的两个时代有明显差异
3. **语气恰当**：根据语境选择肯定或否定态度

## 🔄 相关句型

## 📝 练习题

### 翻译练习
1. 智能手机普及的时代已经一去不复返了。
2. 人们可以不受限制地获取信息的时代已经一去不复返了。
3. 学生可以不用电脑完成作业的时代已经一去不复返了。

### 参考答案
1. *Long gone are the days when smartphones were not widely used.*
2. *Long gone are the days when people had unlimited access to information.*
3. *Long gone are the days when students could complete assignments without computers.*
