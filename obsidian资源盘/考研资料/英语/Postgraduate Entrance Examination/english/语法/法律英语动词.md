# 法律英语常用动词

## 📚 概述

法律英语中有一些特定的动词搭配，表达"限制、阻止、禁止"等含义，这些动词在考研英语和法律文本中频繁出现。

## 🔧 核心动词结构

### 1. constrain...from... 结构

**含义：** 限制...做某事

**结构：** constrain + 宾语 + from + doing

**原句：** employment protection laws constrain business owners from dismissing under-performing managers

**中文意思：** 就业保护法限制企业主解雇表现不佳的管理者

**例句：**
- *Regulations constrain companies from polluting the environment.*
- *The policy constrains employees from sharing confidential information.*
- *Laws constrain individuals from engaging in illegal activities.*

### 2. prevent...from... 结构

**含义：** 阻止...做某事

**结构：** prevent + 宾语 + from + doing

**例句：**
- *The law prevents employers from firing workers without cause.*
- *Safety measures prevent accidents from happening.*
- *Vaccines prevent people from getting diseases.*

### 3. prohibit...from... 结构

**含义：** 禁止...做某事

**结构：** prohibit + 宾语 + from + doing

**例句：**
- *The law prohibits smoking in public places.*
- *Regulations prohibit companies from monopolizing the market.*
- *The contract prohibits employees from working for competitors.*

### 4. restrict...from... 结构

**含义：** 限制...做某事

**结构：** restrict + 宾语 + from + doing

**例句：**
- *The policy restricts access to sensitive information.*
- *Laws restrict the sale of alcohol to minors.*
- *Regulations restrict the use of certain chemicals.*

## 📊 动词对比表

| 动词 | 语气强度 | 正式程度 | 常用搭配 | 例句 |
|------|----------|----------|----------|------|
| **constrain** | 中等 | 高 | from + doing | *constrain from dismissing* |
| **prevent** | 强 | 中 | from + doing | *prevent from firing* |
| **prohibit** | 很强 | 很高 | from + doing | *prohibit from smoking* |
| **restrict** | 中等 | 高 | from + doing / to + noun | *restrict from access* |

## 🎯 使用要点

### 1. from 的用法

**必须保留：**
- 在法律英语和正式文体中，from 通常必须保留
- from 不能省略，否则语法错误

**正确：**
- ✅ *constrain employers from firing workers*
- ✅ *prevent companies from polluting*

**错误：**
- ❌ *constrain employers firing workers*
- ❌ *prevent companies polluting*

### 2. doing 的形式

**动名词：**
- from 后面必须接动名词（doing）
- 不能接不定式（to do）
- 不能接动词原形

**正确：**
- ✅ *from dismissing under-performing managers*
- ✅ *from firing workers without cause*

**错误：**
- ❌ *from dismiss under-performing managers*
- ❌ *from to fire workers*

### 3. 被动语态

**主动语态：**
- *The law constrains employers from firing workers.*

**被动语态：**
- *Employers are constrained from firing workers by the law.*
- *Workers are prevented from being fired without cause.*

## 📝 真题分析

### 原句解析
**原句：** employment protection laws constrain business owners from dismissing under-performing managers

**结构分析：**
- **主语：** employment protection laws
- **谓语：** constrain
- **宾语：** business owners
- **补语：** from dismissing under-performing managers

**语法要点：**
1. constrain...from... 固定搭配
2. from + doing 结构
3. 法律英语的正式表达

### 类似真题
1. *The regulations prevent companies from discharging pollutants.*
2. *Laws prohibit discrimination against protected groups.*
3. *Policies restrict access to confidential information.*

## 🔄 同义转换

### 1. 主动变被动

**主动：** *The law constrains employers from firing workers.*
**被动：** *Employers are constrained from firing workers by the law.*

### 2. 同义替换

**constrain → prevent：**
- *constrain employers from firing* → *prevent employers from firing*

**constrain → prohibit：**
- *constrain illegal activities* → *prohibit illegal activities*

**constrain → restrict：**
- *constrain access* → *restrict access*

## 🎯 考试重点

### 1. 搭配记忆

**重点搭配：**
- constrain...from...
- prevent...from...
- prohibit...from...
- restrict...from...

**记忆口诀：**
"法律四动词，from doing 后，阻止限制禁，搭配要记准"

### 2. 易错点

**常见错误：**
- ❌ *constrain employers to fire*（错误搭配）
- ❌ *prevent employers firing*（缺少 from）
- ❌ *prohibit employers fire*（错误形式）

**正确形式：**
- ✅ *constrain employers from firing*
- ✅ *prevent employers from firing*
- ✅ *prohibit employers from firing*

### 3. 语境判断

**法律语境：**
- 正式程度高
- 语气较强
- from 必须保留

**日常语境：**
- 可以稍微灵活
- from 有时可省略（非正式）

## 📚 练习题

### 选择题
1. The new law _____ companies _____ discharging waste into rivers.
   A) constrains, from
   B) prevents, from
   C) prohibits, from
   D) all of the above

2. Employees _____ from sharing confidential information.
   A) are constrained
   B) are prevented
   C) are prohibited
   D) all of the above

### 填空题
1. Regulations _____ (constrain) businesses _____ exploiting workers.
2. The policy _____ (prevent) discrimination _____ occurring.

### 答案
1. D) all of the above
2. D) all of the above
3. constrain, from
4. prevents, from
