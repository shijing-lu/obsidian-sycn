# 动词用法总览

## 📚 动词 + 宾语 + 补语结构

### 1. 动词 + 宾语 + from + doing

| 动词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **prevent** | 阻止 | *prevent employers from firing* |
| **stop** | 停止 | *stop workers from striking* |
| **keep** | 使...保持 | *keep students from cheating* |
| **discourage** | 劝阻 | *discourage smoking* |

#### 重点：prevent 的用法
- **prevent + 宾语 + from + doing**：阻止某人做某事
- **from 的用法**：正式文体必须保留
- **真题例句**：*The law prevents employers from firing workers without cause.*

### 2. 动词 + 宾语 + to do

| 动词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **ask** | 请求 | *ask students to study* |
| **tell** | 告诉 | *tell him to leave* |
| **force** | 强迫 | *force workers to work* |
| **allow** | 允许 | *allow students to leave* |

### 3. 动词 + 宾语 + doing

| 动词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **catch** | 抓住 | *catch him cheating* |
| **find** | 发现 | *find him sleeping* |
| **see** | 看见 | *see him running* |
| **hear** | 听见 | *hear him singing* |

## 🎯 考试重点动词

### 法律英语常用动词

#### prevent 系列
```
prevent + 宾语 + from + doing
- The law prevents employers from firing workers.
- Regulations prevent companies from polluting.
```

#### prohibit 系列
```
prohibit + 宾语 + from + doing
- The law prohibits discrimination.
- Rules prohibit smoking in public.
```

#### forbid 系列
```
forbid + 宾语 + to do
- The teacher forbids students to use phones.
- The law forbids companies to pollute.
```

### 商业英语常用动词

#### encourage 系列
```
encourage + 宾语 + to do
- The policy encourages investment.
- The program encourages innovation.
```

#### require 系列
```
require + 宾语 + to do
- The job requires employees to work hard.
- The law requires companies to report.
```

## 📖 易混淆动词对比

### prevent vs. prohibit vs. forbid

| 动词 | 结构 | 语气 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|------|
| **prevent** | from + doing | 温和 | *prevent from harming* |
| **prohibit** | from + doing | 正式 | *prohibit from entering* |
| **forbid** | to do | 强硬 | *forbid to smoke* |

### stop vs. prevent

| 动词 | 区别 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **stop** | 停止正在进行的动作 | *stop him from talking* |
| **prevent** | 阻止将要发生的动作 | *prevent him from falling* |

## 🔍 语法分析技巧

### 识别动词结构
```
1. 找主语 → 2. 找谓语动词 → 3. 分析宾语 → 4. 分析补语
```

### 真题分析示例
**原句：** The personal grievance provisions prevent an employer from firing an employee without good cause.

**分析步骤：**
1. 主语：The personal grievance provisions
2. 谓语：prevent
3. 宾语：an employer
4. 补语：from firing an employee without good cause
5. 结构：prevent + 宾语 + from + doing

## 📚 练习题

### 选择题
1. The new law _____ companies _____ polluting the environment.
   A) prevents, from  B) stops, to  C) keeps, from  D) both A and C

2. The teacher _____ students _____ using phones in class.
   A) prevents, from  B) forbids, to  C) prohibits, from  D) both B and C

### 答案
1. D) both A and C
2. D) both B and C
