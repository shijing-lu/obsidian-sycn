
$$
\lim_{x \to 0} \left[ \frac{1}{\ln\left(x + \sqrt{1 + x^2}\right)} - \frac{1}{\ln(1 + x)} \right]
$$

**解答：**

当 $x \to 0$ 时，

$$
\ln(1 + x) \sim x
$$

$$
\ln\left(x + \sqrt{1 + x^2}\right) \sim x + \frac{x^2}{2}
$$

则

$$
\frac{1}{\ln\left(x + \sqrt{1 + x^2}\right)} = \frac{1}{x + \frac{x^2}{2} + o(x^2)} = \frac{1}{x} \cdot \frac{1}{1 + \frac{x}{2} + o(x)} = \frac{1}{x} \left(1 - \frac{x}{2} + o(x)\right) = \frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{2} + o(1)
$$

$$
\frac{1}{\ln(1 + x)} = \frac{1}{x + o(x)} = \frac{1}{x} + o(1)
$$

相减得

$$
\left( \frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{2} + o(1) \right) - \left( \frac{1}{x} + o(1) \right) = -\frac{1}{2} + o(1)
$$

因此

$$
\lim_{x \to 0} \left[ \frac{1}{\ln\left(x + \sqrt{1 + x^2}\right)} - \frac{1}{\ln(1 + x)} \right] = -\frac{1}{2}
$$
