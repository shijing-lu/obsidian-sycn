**解**：令 $t = \frac{1}{x}$，则 $t \to 0$

$$
\text{原式} = \lim_{t \to 0} \left( \sin 2t + \cos t \right)^{\frac{1}{t}}
$$

由 $1^\infty$ 型公式：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \exp\left( \lim_{t \to 0} \frac{1}{t} \left( \sin 2t + \cos t - 1 \right) \right) \\[1em]
&= \exp\left( \lim_{t \to 0} \frac{2t - \frac{t^2}{2}}{t} \right) \\[1em]
&= e^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

**答案**：$\boxed{e^{2}}$
