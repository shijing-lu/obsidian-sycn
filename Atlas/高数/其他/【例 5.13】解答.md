**解**：这是 \(1^\infty\) 型。
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{1}{x} \left( \frac{e^x + e^{2x} + \cdots + e^{nx}}{n} - 1 \right) \\
&= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{1}{x} \cdot \frac{e^x + e^{2x} + \cdots + e^{nx} - n}{n} \\
&= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{1}{n} \cdot \frac{(1 + 2 + \cdots + n)x + o(x)}{x} \\
&= \frac{1}{n} \cdot \frac{n(n+1)}{2} = \frac{n+1}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$
故原式$= e^{\frac{n+1}{2}}$
**答案**：
$$
\boxed{e^{\frac{n+1}{2}}}
$$
