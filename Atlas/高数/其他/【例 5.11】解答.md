**解**：令 $x = \frac{1}{n}$，则 $x \to 0$
$$
\text{原式} = \lim_{x \to 0} \left( \frac{\tan x}{x} \right)^{\frac{1}{x^2}}
$$
由 $1^\infty$ 型公式：
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \exp\left( \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{1}{x^2} \left( \frac{\tan x}{x} - 1 \right) \right) \\[1em]
&= \exp\left( \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\tan x - x}{x^3} \right) \\[1em]
&= \exp\left( \frac{1}{3} \right) = e^{\frac{1}{3}}
\end{aligned}
$$
**答案**：$\boxed{e^{\frac{1}{3}}}$
