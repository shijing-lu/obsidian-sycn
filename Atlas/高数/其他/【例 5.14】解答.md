**解**：
$$
\begin{aligned}
\left(1 + \frac{1}{x}\right)^{x^2} &= e^{x^2 \ln\left(1 + \frac{1}{x}\right)} \\
&= e^{x^2 \left( \frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{2x^2} + o\left(\frac{1}{x^2}\right) \right)} \\
&= e^{x - \frac{1}{2} + o(1)} = e^x \cdot e^{-\frac{1}{2} + o(1)}
\end{aligned}
$$
故
$$
\frac{e^x}{\left(1 + \frac{1}{x}\right)^{x^2}} = \frac{e^x}{e^x \cdot e^{-\frac{1}{2} + o(1)}} = e^{\frac{1}{2} + o(1)} \to e^{\frac{1}{2}}
$$
**答案**：
$$
\boxed{e^{\frac{1}{2}}}
$$
