**有理运算法则**
设 $u(x),\nu(x)$ 都可导，则
+ $(u\pm v)^{\prime}=u^{\prime}\pm v^{\prime}$
+ $(uv)^{\prime}=u^{\prime}v+uv^{\prime}$
+ $(\frac\mu\nu)^{\prime}=\frac{\mu^{\prime}\nu-\nu^{\prime}\mu}{\nu^2}\quad(\nu\neq0)$

**推广：**

(1) $[u_1(x) \pm u_2(x) \pm \cdots \pm u_n(x)]' = u_1'(x) \pm u_2'(x) \pm \cdots \pm u_n'(x)$
(2) $(u_1 u_2 \cdots u_n)' = u_1' u_2 \cdots u_n + u_1 u_2' \cdots u_n + \cdots + u_1 u_2 \cdots u_n'$
