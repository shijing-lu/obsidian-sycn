设参数方程
$$
\begin{cases}
x = x(t) \\
y = y(t)
\end{cases}
$$
满足 $x, y$ 在 $t \in (\alpha, \beta)$ 均可导，且 $x'(t) \neq 0$，则参数方程确定的函数可导，且有：

**一阶导数**
$$
\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{\frac{dy}{dt}}{\frac{dx}{dt}} = \frac{y'(t)}{x'(t)}
$$

**二阶导数**
$$
\frac{d^2 y}{dx^2} = \frac{\frac{d}{dt}\left( \frac{dy}{dx} \right)}{\frac{dx}{dt}} = \frac{y''(t)x'(t) - y'(t)x''(t)}{[x'(t)]^3}
$$
