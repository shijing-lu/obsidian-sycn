**解**：令 $I = \int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sin^2 x}{1+e^x} \, dx$
由区间再现公式（$a+b=0$）：
$$
I = \int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sin^2(-x)}{1+e^{-x}} \, dx = \int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sin^2 x}{1+e^{-x}} \, dx = \int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{e^x \sin^2 x}{1+e^x} \, dx
$$
两式相加：
$$
2I = \int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \left( \frac{1}{1+e^x} + \frac{e^x}{1+e^x} \right) \sin^2 x \, dx = \int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^2 x \, dx
$$
所以：
$$
I = \frac{1}{2} \int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^2 x \, dx = \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^2 x \, dx = \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{1 - \cos 2x}{2} \, dx = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{\pi}{2} = \frac{\pi}{4}
$$
故：
$$
\boxed{I = \frac{\pi}{4}}
$$
