**解**：令 $I = \int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} (x+1) \min\left\{\frac{1}{2}, \cos x\right\} \, dx$
注意到 $\min\left\{\frac{1}{2}, \cos x\right\}$ 是偶函数（$\cos x$ 为偶，常数函数为偶，min 保持偶性）。
由区间再现公式（$a+b=0$）：
$$
I = \int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} (-x+1) \min\left\{\frac{1}{2}, \cos(-x)\right\} \, dx = \int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} (-x+1) \min\left\{\frac{1}{2}, \cos x\right\} \, dx
$$
两式相加：
$$
2I = \int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \big[(x+1) + (-x+1)\big] \min\left\{\frac{1}{2}, \cos x\right\} \, dx = \int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} 2 \min\left\{\frac{1}{2}, \cos x\right\} \, dx
$$
所以：
$$
I = \int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \min\left\{\frac{1}{2}, \cos x\right\} \, dx = 2 \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \min\left\{\frac{1}{2}, \cos x\right\} \, dx
$$
由于 $\cos x$ 在 $[0, \frac{\pi}{3}]$ 上 $\ge \frac{1}{2}$，在 $[\frac{\pi}{3}, \frac{\pi}{2}]$ 上 $\le \frac{1}{2}$，所以：
$$
\min\left\{\frac{1}{2}, \cos x\right\} = \begin{cases} \frac{1}{2}, & 0 \le x \le \frac{\pi}{3} \\ \cos x, & \frac{\pi}{3} \le x \le \frac{\pi}{2} \end{cases}
$$
于是：
$$
I = 2\left( \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{3}} \frac{1}{2} \, dx + \int_{\frac{\pi}{3}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \cos x \, dx \right) = 2\left( \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{\pi}{3} + \left[\sin x\right]_{\frac{\pi}{3}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \right) = 2\left( \frac{\pi}{6} + 1 - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \right)
$$
故：
$$
\boxed{I = \frac{\pi}{3} + 2 - \sqrt{3}}
$$
