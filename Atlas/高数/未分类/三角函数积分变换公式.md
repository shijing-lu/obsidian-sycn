设 \( f(x) \) 在区间 \( [0,1] \) 上连续, 则
### 1) $\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} f(\sin x) dx = \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} f(\cos x) dx$
证明: 由区间再现公式,
$$
\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} f(\sin x) dx = \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} f\left( \sin \left(\frac{\pi}{2} - x\right) \right) dx = \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} f(\cos x) dx
$$
### 2) $\int_{0}^{\pi} f(\sin x) dx = 2 \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} f(\sin x) dx, \quad \int_{0}^{\pi} f(|\cos x|) dx = 2 \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} f(\cos x) dx$

证明: (只证 \( f(sin x) \) 情形)
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_{0}^{\pi} f(\sin x) dx &= \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} f(\sin x) dx + \int_{\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\pi} f(\sin x) dx \\
&= \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} f(\sin x) dx + \int_{\frac{\pi}{2}}^{0} f\left[ \sin (\pi - t) \right] d(\pi - t) \quad (\text{令 } \pi - x = t) \\
&= \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} f(\sin x) dx + \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} f(\sin t) dt = 2 \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} f(\sin x) dx
\end{aligned}
$$
### 3)$\int_{0}^{\pi} x f(\sin x) dx = \frac{\pi}{2} \int_{0}^{\pi} f(\sin x) dx$
证明: 由区间再现公式,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_{0}^{\pi} x f(\sin x) dx &= \int_{0}^{\pi} (\pi - x) f\left[ \sin (\pi - x) \right] dx \\
&= \pi \int_{0}^{\pi} f(\sin x) dx - \int_{0}^{\pi} x f(\sin x) dx
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\Rightarrow \quad \int_{0}^{\pi} x f(\sin x) dx = \frac{\pi}{2} \int_{0}^{\pi} f(\sin x) dx
$$
注: 在 \( [0, \pi] \) 上 \( f(\cos^2 x), f(|\cos x|) \) 可以看作 \( f(\sin x) \), \( f(\cos x) \) 不可看作 \( f(\sin x) \).
### 2) 华里士公式:
$$
\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^n x dx = \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \cos^n x dx = 
\begin{cases} 
\frac{(n-1)!!}{n!!} \cdot \frac{\pi}{2}, & n \ge 2 \text{ 为正偶数}, \\ 
\frac{(n-1)!!}{n!!}, & n \ge 3 \text{ 为正奇数}.
\end{cases}
$$

$$
\int_{0}^{2\pi} \sin^n x dx = \int_{0}^{2\pi} \cos^n x dx = 
\begin{cases} 
4 \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^n x dx, & n \text{ 为正偶数}, \\
0, & n \text{ 为正奇数}.
\end{cases}
$$
